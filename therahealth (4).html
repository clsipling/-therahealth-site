About Us

A space built on belonging

At TheraHealth, we believe that healing happens when you feel truly seen. We've built a practice that celebrates neurodivergent minds and affirms every gender identity and sexual orientation — not as an afterthought, but as our foundation.

Our therapists bring lived experience and specialized training to serve LGBTQ+ individuals, those with ADHD and autism, and anyone who has felt like they don't quite fit in traditional therapy spaces.

No masking required. No need to explain yourself. Just real, compassionate support.

Our Services

Care tailored to you

A range of affirming therapeutic services, delivered in-person and via telehealth across Pennsylvania.

🧩

ADHD & Autism Support

Strength-based therapy that works with your brain, not against it. Practical strategies, self-advocacy, and genuine understanding of neurodivergent experience.

🏳️‍🌈

LGBTQ+ Identity & Affirmation

Affirming support for identity exploration, coming out, gender dysphoria, and navigating the world as your full self.

🌿

Anxiety & Depression

Evidence-based approaches including ACT, DBT, and somatic work — adapted for neurodivergent brains and LGBTQ+ lived experience.

🫂

Trauma & PTSD

Gentle, paced trauma processing with specialized focus on minority stress, family rejection, and the compounding effects of marginalization.

💑

Couples & Relationship Therapy

Affirming couples therapy for queer couples, polyamorous relationships, and partners navigating neurodivergent dynamics.

📱

Telehealth Sessions

Secure, comfortable virtual therapy from your own space — ideal for sensory sensitivities, mobility needs, or busy schedules.

Meet Your Therapist

Founder & Clinical Director

Crystal Sipling, MA, LPC

Crystal founded TheraHealth because she believes everyone deserves a therapist who truly sees them — not just their diagnosis or identity, but the whole, complex, beautiful person they are.

With specialized training in LGBTQ+ affirming care and neurodivergent experiences, Crystal brings both clinical expertise and genuine warmth to every session. She is committed to creating a space where you never have to mask, explain yourself, or shrink to fit in.

Whether you're exploring your identity, navigating an ADHD or autism diagnosis, or simply looking for support that finally feels right — Crystal is here for it.
